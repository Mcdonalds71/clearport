Privacy &
terms.

Clearport Technologies Limited operates in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Customs Service Act 2023. The workspace processes the shipment references, customs entries, review notes, and PDFs submitted by a clearing desk.

Purpose
Use these records for pre-arrival cargo document comparison, ten-digit ECOWAS CET tariff review, and internal PAAR preparation.
Document custody
Signed-in desks access only their own consignments. Clearport never sells or trains public foundation models on customer commercial invoices or supplier unit pricing.
Statutory filing responsibility
Confirm classifications, SONCAP/NAFDAC permit validity, duty rates, and filing requirements with a licensed specialist before submission on NICIS II or B’Odogwu.
Privacy enquiries
Contact desk@clearport.ai to discuss data access, correction, statutory retention, or deletion.
